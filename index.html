About Us

DMC Chorus

DMC Chorus is a church choir dedicated to sacred music and the celebration of faith through song. Rooted in our devotion to the Santo Niño, we serve the Church and community through music, worship, and fellowship.

We come together not only to sing, but to serve by enriching liturgical celebrations, inspiring the faithful, and sharing the joy of music with others.

Guided by faith, friendship, and a shared love for music, we continue to grow as a community committed to serving God and others, always striving to live out our mission of Doing More for Christ.
